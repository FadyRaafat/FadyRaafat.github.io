PillPal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

No account, no cloud, no tracking — everything stays on your device.

Your medication list can reveal a lot about your health. PillPal is built so that information never has to leave your hands. This policy explains what PillPal stores, what it sends, and what it never does.

What we store

Your profiles, medications, schedules, and dose history live in a private database on your device only. Backups are files you create yourself and keep wherever you choose. PillPal has no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no server that holds your data.

What we send

The only connections PillPal ever makes are:

  • Downloading a small public settings file (used to configure the app remotely — it contains no personal data and no data is sent with the request beyond a standard web request).
  • On the free plan, loading ads (see below).

No data about you, your medications, or your dose history is ever sent anywhere.

About ads

The free plan shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Ads never receive your medications, schedules, or history. Google may process device information (such as advertising identifiers) to serve ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. Going Premium removes ads entirely — the ad SDK is never initialized, and nothing ad-related runs or loads.

Permissions

PillPal asks for notification and alarm permissions solely to deliver your medication reminders on time. These permissions are never used for anything else.

Children

PillPal is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — it collects no personal information at all.
