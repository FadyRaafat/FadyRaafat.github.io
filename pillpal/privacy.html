PillPal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

PillPal is a medication reminder app for you and your family. It is built so that your health information never has to leave your hands.

Everything stays on your device

Your profiles, medications, schedules, and dose history live in a private database on your device only. PillPal has no accounts, no cloud sync, no server, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or share any data about you. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Backups are files you create through the system file picker and keep wherever you choose. They never pass through us.

The only network connections

The only connection PillPal ever makes is downloading a public settings file (a small configuration file hosted on this website, requested without any identifiers), and, on the free plan, loading ads. No data about you is ever sent.

Ads on the free plan

The free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads never receive your medications, schedules, or history. Google may process device information to serve and measure ads — see Google's advertising privacy information and how Google uses data in AdMob apps.

Going Premium removes ads entirely: the ad SDK is never initialized and nothing ad-related runs or loads.

Permissions PillPal asks for

Notifications and exact alarms are used solely to remind you to take your medication at the times you choose. They trigger locally on your device and involve no network activity.

Children

PillPal is a general-audience app. Family profiles (for example, a child's medication schedule managed by a parent) are stored only on the caregiver's device, like all other data.
